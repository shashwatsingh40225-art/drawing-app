# FINAL PRODUCT PURPOSE — Kin

> **Status:** AUTHORITATIVE — All other documents defer to this.
> **Date:** 2026-09-12

---

## What Kin Is

Kin is a **personal digital art room and creative companion** for casual but consistent hobby artists.

It is an intimate, calming space where a person can:

1. Enter a pleasant personal creative space
2. Upload and preserve their drawings
3. Build a personal digital sketchbook
4. Organize artwork with lightweight metadata
5. Revisit older work
6. Compare related drawings or versions over time
7. Reflect on visible creative development
8. Save and explore artistic inspiration
9. Display selected artwork in a personal virtual art space
10. Enjoy the process of making art without feeling managed by software

---

## The Central Product Principle

> **Kin should help a hobby artist preserve, revisit, understand, and enjoy their creative journey.**

Every feature, technical decision, and design choice must serve this principle. A feature that is technically impressive but weakens this product identity must be rejected or postponed.

---

## What Kin Is Not

Kin must never become:

| Anti-pattern | Why it violates Kin's purpose |
|---|---|
| Professional art-studio management system | Kin's users are hobbyists, not professionals managing clients and deadlines |
| Art-business CRM | No commercial dimension — this is a personal space |
| Enterprise dashboard | No KPIs, metrics, or performance tracking |
| Productivity application | Art is not a task to be optimized |
| Social network | Kin is private by default — no followers, likes, or public feeds |
| Public art marketplace | No selling, no pricing, no commerce |
| Complex AI research platform | AI serves the artist; it is not the product |
| Large-scale SaaS product | 10-100 users, not 10,000 |
| Feature-heavy technical demonstration | Every feature must earn its place through user value |

---

## The Feeling Kin Should Create

Kin should feel:

- **Intimate** — like a personal journal, not a public gallery
- **Artistic** — the interface should honor the artwork, not compete with it
- **Calm** — no urgency, no notifications, no anxiety-inducing metrics
- **Personal** — your art, your space, your pace
- **Visually distinctive** — not a generic dashboard; it has a clear aesthetic voice

---

## Target User

A person who:

- Draws, sketches, or paints regularly as a personal creative practice
- Uses pencils, pens, markers, watercolors, acrylics, or digital tools
- Wants to preserve and organize their creative output
- Wants to look back at their journey and see how they've grown
- Is NOT a professional artist managing a business
- Is NOT looking for social validation or audience growth
- Values privacy and personal reflection

## Existing Visual Identity

Kin has an established, distinctive visual identity built from 20 original artworks by the creator. This identity includes:

- **Typography:** Fraunces (display serif) + Inter (humanist sans)
- **Palette:** Warm earth tones (sable, rust, aubergine, cream) with magenta and teal accents
- **Motifs:** Double-outline offset, ruled-notebook backgrounds, concentric discs, feather dividers
- **Artwork presentation:** Cream museum-mat backgrounds, uncropped images, generous padding
- **Brand mark:** Custom eye/orb motif (ART-05)

This visual identity must be preserved. It is a strength, not a liability.
